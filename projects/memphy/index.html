Memphy

Year2026
FormatWebsite, merch shop, CMS
Clientmemphy, model, DJ and producer (Brooklyn)
StoreSA1NT ONE (Shopify)
Design & DevHumberto Gesser
StackVanilla JS, Three.js, Vercel, Shopify Storefront API
Sitememphy.co
memphy.co is the site of memphy, a Brooklyn model, DJ and producer, built in the interface of a 2010-era webcam site. Visitors land in a live room: memphy performs her single on one screen while fans take turns on the line on the other, and a chat scrolls in fifteen languages. Around it sit her modeling, music, gigs and a merch shop, all in the same vernacular. The parody carries real information: every credit, comment and press link on the site is sourced.
The site is one static HTML file, built in six weeks. One player bar drives native video, YouTube and SoundCloud. The shop checks out through her label's Shopify, and an editor lets her team update the site themselves, publishing each change as a Git commit.
