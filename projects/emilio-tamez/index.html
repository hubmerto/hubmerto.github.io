Emilio Tamez

digital · 2026
Year2026
FormatPortfolio website, editor
ClientEmilio Tamez, photographer (New York)
Design & DevHumberto Gesser
StackNext.js, React, CSS Modules, Vercel
Siteemilio-tamez-site.vercel.app
Emilio Tamez wanted a site where nothing performs except the photographs, so it behaves like an object you turn in your hands. Twenty-four works hang on a single column of numbers with no beginning and no end. "If I just roll my finger fast, it keeps running until it loses traction," she said, and the column became a flywheel. Each time it comes back around, a work shows a different face, changed while no one is looking.
Nothing here feels like loading a page. Click a photograph and it flies into its gallery; close the project and the last picture you looked at flies home and stays on as the cover. Fashion waits behind one switch, the same instrument tuned dark. She runs the site herself, and published thirty times on her first day.
