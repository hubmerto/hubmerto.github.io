Strelitzia

3d · blender · 2023
ClientSelf-initiated
Year2023
Department3D
TypologySpec project, material study
SoftwareBlender
OutputTurntable video loops
The portfolio study takes a single botanical subject, the bird of paradise, and strips it of its natural palette to rebuild it under museum conditions: black lacquer on the outer sepals, deep gold on the inner petals, condensation held on the surface as if kept behind glass at the wrong temperature.
The species was chosen for its geometry rather than its color, asymmetric and angular, a hard spathe and blade-like sepals fused into a structure that reads as apparatus rather than ornament, supplying sharp creases, long curvature and thin edges where light breaks. Each material is tuned against that geometry. The lacquer runs near-mirror on the ridges and falls off fast into the creases, so form is read through reflection rather than shading, while the gold sits warmer and softer to separate the inner structure from the shell.
Condensation gathers dense in the crevices and thins across the exposed faces, giving the gloss a scale reference. The whole is lit as product photography, a controlled key against a black surround so the lacquer returns the light source as a readable shape rather than a glow, then rendered as seamless turntable loops that show every face of the material in sequence.
